## 1. Student identity

CASA School does not require a school admission number.

Every student always receives a globally unique CASA Student ID generated by the platform:

`CASA-STU-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX`

The school's own admission number, student number, registration number, or equivalent is optional.

A school that already has such identifiers may retain them.

A school that does not use them does not need to invent one just to use CASA.

Student search should support:

- CASA Student ID;
- optional school student/admission number;
- student name;
- class;
- guardian;
- card serial where appropriate.

## 2. Initial onboarding vs ongoing school operation

### CASA Technical

CASA Technical handles the implementation/rollout layer where required:

- school onboarding;
- initial data import;
- bulk student registration;
- initial face enrollment;
- initial card rollout;
- Scanner PWA provisioning;
- device/terminal diagnostics;
- recovery and hardware replacement support.

CASA Technical is not the day-to-day school registrar.

### School Technician

After handover, each school may appoint one or more staff members with the `SCHOOL_TECHNICIAN` role.

A School Technician is strictly scoped to that school.

The role may operate:

- new student registration;
- student identity correction;
- guardian creation/linking;
- class enrollment;
- future face enrollment/re-enrollment;
- student ID-card issuance/replacement;
- future Scanner PWA/terminal setup and troubleshooting.

The role does not imply:

- school ownership;
- transfer of ownership;
- arbitrary Admin creation/removal;
- cross-school access;
- access to CASA master card templates;
- rewriting immutable attendance/security history.

OWNER and ADMIN remain the school's administrative authority.

## 3. Authentication and CASA Passkeys

Passkeys remain a signature CASA identity capability.

CASA School will support:

- password login;
- passkey login;
- passkey step-up authorization for sensitive operations.

Password authentication must not replace or eliminate passkeys.

Examples of operations intended for passkey step-up include:

- assigning privileged roles;
- issuing/reissuing sensitive identity credentials;
- face enrollment reset;
- terminal registration/revocation;
- privileged attendance corrections;
- security-setting changes.

Passkey credential/challenge implementation is a dedicated authentication phase and must use the same global user identity rather than a parallel account system.

## 4. Scanner PWA

The attendance scanner is a separate installable PWA.

It is not the normal School Admin interface.

Intended deployment:

1. suitable Android tablet/device opens the CASA School Scanner URL;
2. the PWA is installed;
3. the device is provisioned to exactly one school;
4. the device receives a one-time terminal credential;
5. PostgreSQL stores only the terminal credential hash;
6. the terminal operates in a focused attendance/kiosk experience.

## 5. CASA-owned card templates

The master student-card design is CASA intellectual property and an operational control.

Schools and School Technicians must never receive:

- the editable master template;
- template source files;
- layout definitions intended for CASA production;
- a reusable blank card design that lets them reproduce CASA cards independently.

Template assets belong to a CASA-only production surface.

Templates are versioned because card designs may change over time.

A card-production record must retain the exact template version used.

## 6. Secure QR rendering rule

Student cards use opaque random QR credentials.

PostgreSQL stores only SHA-256 hashes.

The raw QR token must not be persisted merely for later printing.

Therefore the production architecture is:

1. active CASA-owned template version is selected;
2. card credential is generated in server memory;
3. QR is rendered into the student's final front/back card artwork while the raw credential is still available;
4. the editable/master template and template source assets remain private to CASA;
5. the finished student card artifact is published under a separate high-entropy public-by-link object key/URL;
6. the public artifact URL is unrelated to the student QR credential and uses no sequential student/card identifier;
7. only the credential hash, card metadata, template-version reference, rendered-artifact reference, and public artifact URL/key persist;
8. the raw QR credential is discarded.

The finished card URL requires no login, school session, CASA session, or role authorization. Possession of the unguessable URL is sufficient to view/download the rendered card.

The public card URL must be:

- high entropy and practically unguessable;
- non-indexable (`noindex`/equivalent);
- excluded from directory listing;
- revocable/rotatable by CASA if exposed;
- separate from the QR credential so rotating the download URL does not change the physical card credential.

If an active template/rendering service is unavailable, production issuance must fail safely rather than persist a recoverable raw token.

## 7. Student onboarding notification to CASA

When a School Technician completes a new student's identity onboarding, CASA should receive an operations event.

The eventual event/outbox should carry non-secret identifiers such as:

- school;
- student;
- CASA Student ID;
- card;
- card-production job;
- onboarding timestamp.

The notification mechanism should use a durable outbox/event model rather than hard-coupling student registration to one email address.

CASA Motherboard/operations can consume these events later.

## 8. Central card-production queue

CASA will have a central production queue across schools.

A production row should be able to expose:

- school name;
- student name;
- CASA Student ID;
- optional school student/admission number;
- date of birth;
- age calculated as of export date;
- gender/sex display value;
- class;
- card serial;
- template version;
- front rendered artifact;
- back rendered artifact;
- public ID Card URL;
- production status;
- onboarding/queued timestamp;
- printed/exported timestamp.

The school-facing application should expose production status only where useful.

It must not expose the master template.

## 9. Batch export

CASA operations should be able to filter and batch production by school/date/status.

The intended export is an Excel-style `.xlsx` production manifest.

Typical columns:

- School;
- Student Name;
- CASA Student ID;
- optional School Student/Admission Number;
- Date of Birth;
- Age (computed at export time, never stored as a permanent age field);
- Gender/Sex;
- Class;
- Card Serial;
- ID Card URL;
- Template Version;
- Production Status;
- Queued At.

`ID Card URL` is clickable and requires no authentication. It resolves to the finished rendered card artifact so CASA can immediately view and download it.

The export may also include a ZIP/folder of the same front/back files for bulk production, with stable filenames keyed by CASA Student ID/card serial.

This replaces manual per-student card design.

The spreadsheet is a production manifest, not the source of truth.

The database remains authoritative.

## 10. Frontend freeze

Existing Registry screens are functional plumbing.

Do not continue visually expanding or polishing the School Admin frontend until the underlying school operations and identity workflows are stable.

The eventual design pass will use the approved CASA visual references:

- strong editorial typography;
- monochrome/neutral composition;
- deliberate asymmetry;
- substantial whitespace;
- precise rules/borders;
- restrained accent usage;
- minimal conventional SaaS-card styling.

No current functional screen should be mistaken for the final CASA School visual design.
## 11. Student departure / sign-out

The target daily presence lifecycle is:

Before a student leaves after school, the student must sign out at any ACTIVE Scanner PWA terminal assigned to that school.

Sign-out must use the same identity principle as arrival:

**Scan ID Ã¢â€ â€™ Verify Face Ã¢â€ â€™ Verify Liveness Ã¢â€ â€™ Verify Departure Time Ã¢â€ â€™ Record Sign-out**

A successful sign-out creates a durable departure record/event and changes the student's daily presence state from on-campus to signed-out.

CASA must then emit a notification event for the student's eligible guardian contacts, for example:

`Dolapo Ayomide has signed out of school and is on the way home.`

The notification event must be emitted through a durable outbox/event mechanism. Attendance recording must not depend on a WhatsApp/SMS/push provider being online at that exact moment.

### Normal dismissal

The attendance policy will include a school-day dismissal/sign-out window.

A normal sign-out is accepted only when that departure window is open.

### Early departure

If a student must leave before the normal dismissal window, CASA must not silently treat the departure as normal.

Early departure will require an authorized school staff override/verification and will retain:

- student;
- date/session;
- departure timestamp;
- terminal;
- identity-verification evidence;
- authorizing membership;
- reason;
- guardian-notification outcome.

### Duplicate and impossible states

The eventual attendance lifecycle must prevent:

- sign-out before a successful check-in/presence record;
- duplicate active sign-outs for the same school day;
- a second normal check-in after final sign-out without an explicit re-entry workflow;
- School A terminals signing out School B students.

Phase 3A currently provides the arrival-oriented persistence foundation.

Before Scanner PWA terminal APIs are finalized, the next attendance implementation phase must extend that foundation to model CHECK_IN and CHECK_OUT explicitly rather than creating a parallel departure subsystem.
